# Ek Tap Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 September 2026*

Ek Tap is a collection of offline one-button games made by oyekamal.

## What the app stores on your device
- Your best score for each game and a small settings file. These stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

## What the app sends over the internet
- **Update check:** once every few hours the app asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release number, so it can offer an Update button. No personal data is included.
- **Ads:** the app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The app shows only ads rated G (general audiences). It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Children
Ek Tap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
